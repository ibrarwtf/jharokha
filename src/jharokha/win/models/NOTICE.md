# PP-OCRv6_rec_small.onnx

The text recognition model of PaddleOCR's PP-OCRv6 (small), as converted to ONNX and
distributed by RapidOCR 3.10.0 (`rapidocr/models/PP-OCRv6_rec_small.onnx`, source
https://www.modelscope.cn/models/RapidAI/RapidOCR/resolve/v3.9.2/onnx/PP-OCRv6/rec/PP-OCRv6_rec_small.onnx).
SHA-256 `6f327246b50388f3c176ae304bd95767ea6dc0c9ae92153ef8cbe210b3c14884`. Unchanged; its
character dictionary is in the model's metadata (`character`).

Converted from an official PaddleOCR model to the indicated inference format and packaged
for use with RapidOCR. The upstream model weights are copyright Baidu and/or the applicable
PaddleOCR rights holders and are licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0 (`LICENSE`
in this folder). Upstream: https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR,
https://huggingface.co/PaddlePaddle/PP-OCRv6_small_rec_onnx.
